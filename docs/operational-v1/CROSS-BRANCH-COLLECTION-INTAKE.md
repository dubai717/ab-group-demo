# Cross-branch cash collection review

An active employee with the explicit company grant can look up the remaining balance of one invoice by invoice and legal company ID. The response contains the invoice number, currency, balance, and the employee's verified receiving site. It contains no customer record or journal data.

The employee reports cash actually received against that invoice and site. PostgreSQL validates the exact relationship and amount and permits the employee to see their own reports only while the eligible grant remains active. A report has no accounting effect. An independent active Owner confirms the counted amount. The original transaction then creates the receipt, invoice allocation, posted journal and custody movement in the company that owns the debt. The reporting employee holds the cash custody and the Owner remains the author of the posting. The report can produce only one posting. The existing original handover, recipient confirmation and company bank deposit workflow remains in use.

The SQL is a review candidate. The API defaults to disabled and accepts the isolated fixture database naming convention only. Deployment, broader delegation and exceptions, UI and end-to-end acceptance of the complete company scope remain pending.

The scope composition fixture includes separate card reconciliation, payroll decision and personnel library version relationships. Each relationship validates its original parents in the same tenant. Payroll also verifies employee and month. The original application action checks and current company boundaries remain in effect. The full scope activation gate remains closed while other relationships are unresolved.

The confirmed intake receipt also passed the original physical cash handover, independent recipient count, and deposit confirmation to the owning company's bank account. A bank account belonging to another company was rejected. The reporter's database access to the posting link exposes only existence, with no receipt batch ID or approver ID accessible through that link.

Personnel document boundaries now distinguish a legal employer from temporary assignments. The scoped fixture preserves a worker's own document access with an empty company scope and verifies that an assignment can expose employee identity without exposing their document versions or metadata. These rules cover personnel-owned documents and library versions. Other private or shared document ownership models need separate rules before the overall scope gate can open.

The intake candidate requires the company and customer row policies in the same isolated database. The acceptance fixture compiles the reporter for the receiving-site company only, confirms the invoice of the debt company through the limited workflow, and checks directly as the runtime actor that the debt company's customer, invoice, receipt, and journal rows remain invisible. The previous experimental customer-wide lookup cannot be called by the runtime after the exact-invoice candidate is installed.

The isolated build adds a bilingual account-menu entry for the actual authorized worker or Owner. The worker supplies the exact debt company and invoice IDs, confirms physical cash, and receives a report ID to share with the Owner. The Owner reopens that report and enters the independently counted amount before posting. A lost response retries the same payload and request key. The browser contract is tested in Arabic at phone width and English at desktop width, with a separate PostgreSQL test for the actual authorization and financial effects.
