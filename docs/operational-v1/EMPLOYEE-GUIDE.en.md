# Quick guide — unpublished branch additions

- Prospects retain their original customer file and separate summary. Source/responsible selection does not grant access. Messaging language is explicit, never inferred from nationality.
- Job standards have their own screen. Moving a section exposes actions without granting them. Select the required actions and review the original permission plan before confirming. Employee access continues to use the existing authorization engine.
- Annual leave plans are tentative. Saving does not approve leave or deduct balances. Add suggested dates, or explain why dates are not decided. Actual leave requests follow the existing approval workflow.
- Failed saves retain your input. Correct the error and retry; a spinner does not prove completion.
- The two-month service policy and WhatsApp contract are isolated computations/tests, not activated operational features.

When approved leave suspends work access, use Leave return self-service on the login screen. This limited portal does not open work data. Submit the actual return date and note, then await authorized confirmation. Confirmation does not clear an independent security restriction; historical holds without evidence need a separate authorized access review.

Authorized staff can open Work calendar from an employee profile. Select the area, company and effective date; click a cell to cycle X, / and blank. Hours & save records explicit hours, holidays and a reason as a dated version. Historical schedules remain preserved. Failed confirmation keeps inputs for a safe retry.

Open Deadline & waiting from the original task. An authorized supervisor records UAE time, a reason and any approved external waiting. You cannot approve your own extension. Task timing does not change invoice or contract due dates; an escalation notice is neither another task nor a financial deduction.

With delegation authority, open the employee profile and Delegate operational
tasks. Optional approved leave fills dates; choose an active available substitute.
Current linkage supports manual, employee-document and case-linked conversation tasks when the substitute already has source authority. Open work returns at
expiry or confirmed return; completed work stays with its actual performer.
Reload saved result recovers a failed display refresh without creating again.

On standard impact review, retain every exception by default or explicitly select additions or denials to remove, with a reason. Preview the selection impact before confirming. Unselected exceptions and company scope remain unchanged; removing a denial restores only original authority; downstream grants require separate review.

From the original conversation, link a task and select its employee and date. An optional exact UAE time must match the date. If the response fails, retry in the same form. An existing task is not duplicated or silently rescheduled; use its authorized clock action for changes.

In the original conversation, create a follow-up topic for a payment claim, dispute or complaint. Another authorized participant can continue handling it. Open its row for original history or link an existing-workflow follow-up task. Administrative resolution never settles debt or completes the task. No-response closure awaits verified deadline/follow-up support. Retry uncertain saves in the same form with the original values.

Owner: Review two-month reference in the existing customer profile shows an original-source simulation. Refresh manually after payment. Review required identifies unresolved source facts; it is not payment or debt release. Service blocking is not activated in this package.

Owner: Individual denials in Employee permissions is a separate mode: checking an action there restricts it. Enter a reason and review before/after. If confirmation is uncertain, retry the same request from the same screen. A reporting reference outside the visible directory keeps its current value without exposing hidden account details.

Owner: Reset to job standard previews saved individual exceptions and requires a reason and confirmation. Role/work scope stay unchanged. Verify uncertain outcomes from the same review; closing stays held until verification. Canceling preview retains the previous parent draft. Table/chart previews in indicator settings use the reviewer’s authorized data; chart refresh is manual.

### Employee indicator inheritance
In employee permissions, Inherited follows the job standard. Individual addition and Individual denial remain when that standard changes. Inherit standard clears only the selected item's draft exception: review and confirm to save, or close to cancel. Selecting an indicator grants no additional data authority. Standard review shows each affected account's indicator counts and retained exceptions; refresh the preview if an intervening change makes it stale.

Individual indicator inheritance requires approved database protection. If the guard notice is shown, previous-format settings remain available without automatic conversion. A newer-format record with incomplete protection is read-only until protection is restored; do not bypass the notice.

Owner: the dashboard Backup status section reads only after Check backup status. An unconfigured source does not establish absence of backups. Review capture/test dates and storage location; artifact presence does not prove full recoverability. This section has no restore or download action.

Existing customer/vehicle import: choose CSV UTF-8, Excel or Access, then company and file. Failed attempts retain the file with a clear reference; explicit removal is available. Review mapping, duplicates and company before confirmation. Preview creates no customer or vehicle and imports no balances. Real operating-data import requires final approval and an approved backup.

To follow up a completed conversation-topic task, open Topic follow-up task, explicitly select the completed parent, then the employee, deadline and expected outcome. Leaving the parent blank preserves the original link. If a different successor exists, open the current task for review. Completing a call does not collect the debt.

Owner can open View recorded centres from the employee permission page, then add a centre or record a dated change from its history. Choose actual companies/location, effective date and reason, then preview before confirming. This grants no employee rights. Retry the same operation after an unconfirmed outcome; after an acknowledged save, use verification without another save. Previous snapshots remain immutable.

From employee permissions, open Primary work location. Physical centre is separate from granted companies. Choose an effective date and centre or Unassigned, enter a reason and preview before confirmation. Retry the same operation after an unconfirmed outcome. After acknowledged save, use Verify save without another write. Future and historical snapshots remain retained.

In a conversation topic, “Response deadline” opens the responsible employee/reason form. The internal review date uses the recorded employee calendar and the next three scheduled working dates, excluding the recording date. This is not evidence of customer delivery. An incomplete calendar requires review and retains your input. Reopening needs a fresh review date; previous history remains. No-response closure and external sending remain disabled.

“Document expiry” shows a preview30 days before expiry and on expiry for the current version. It does not send a message or charge a fee. A replacement changes the current-version dates. A preview proves neither delivery nor document authenticity. Expiry does not prevent accepting payment.

Owner only: choose “Reminder schedule” on a document card, select company/current type/customer-type where available, offsets, effective date and reason. This configures preview dates and does not enable sending. Retry an uncertain save with the same values; after acknowledged save, retry reads the result only. A future rule does not replace today's schedule early.
