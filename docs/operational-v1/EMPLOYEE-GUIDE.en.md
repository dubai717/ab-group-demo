# Quick guide — unpublished branch additions

- Prospects retain their original customer file and separate summary. Source/responsible selection does not grant access. Messaging language is explicit, never inferred from nationality.
- Job standards have their own screen. Moving a section exposes actions without granting them. Select the required actions and review the original permission plan before confirming. Employee access continues to use the existing authorization engine.
- Annual leave plans are tentative. Saving does not approve leave or deduct balances. Add suggested dates, or explain why dates are not decided. Actual leave requests follow the existing approval workflow.
- Failed saves retain your input. Correct the error and retry; a spinner does not prove completion.
- The two-month service policy and WhatsApp contract are isolated computations/tests, not activated operational features.
