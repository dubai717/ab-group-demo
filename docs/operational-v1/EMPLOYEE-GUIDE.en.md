# Quick guide — unpublished branch additions

- Prospects retain their original customer file and separate summary. Source/responsible selection does not grant access. Messaging language is explicit, never inferred from nationality.
- Job standards have their own screen. Moving a section exposes actions without granting them. Select the required actions and review the original permission plan before confirming. Employee access continues to use the existing authorization engine.
- Annual leave plans are tentative. Saving does not approve leave or deduct balances. Add suggested dates, or explain why dates are not decided. Actual leave requests follow the existing approval workflow.
- Failed saves retain your input. Correct the error and retry; a spinner does not prove completion.
- The two-month service policy and WhatsApp contract are isolated computations/tests, not activated operational features.

When approved leave suspends work access, use Leave return self-service on the login screen. This limited portal does not open work data. Submit the actual return date and note, then await authorized confirmation. Confirmation does not clear an independent security restriction; historical holds without evidence need a separate authorized access review.

Authorized staff can open Work calendar from an employee profile. Select the area, company and effective date; click a cell to cycle X, / and blank. Hours & save records explicit hours, holidays and a reason as a dated version. Historical schedules remain preserved. Failed confirmation keeps inputs for a safe retry.

Open Deadline & waiting from the original task. An authorized supervisor records UAE time, a reason and any approved external waiting. You cannot approve your own extension. Task timing does not change invoice or contract due dates; an escalation notice is neither another task nor a financial deduction.

With delegation authority, open the employee profile and Delegate operational
tasks. Optional approved leave fills dates; choose an active available substitute.
Current linkage supports manual and employee-document tasks. Open work returns at
expiry or confirmed return; completed work stays with its actual performer.
Reload saved result recovers a failed display refresh without creating again.

On standard impact review, retain all additions by default or select explicit additions to remove. Preview the selection impact before confirming. Unselected grants and company scope remain unchanged; downstream grants require separate review.
