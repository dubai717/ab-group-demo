# Owner financial reports — read only, build only

The report reads native personal-card payment splits, their encrypted original classifications, posted double-entry balances, original card-statement lines/matches and signed Owner-settlement/PV sources. It does not infer company income or customer collection from card payments. It creates no journal, invoice, receipt, custody movement or financial fact merely by opening or exporting.

Each authorized company has a separate summary: opening/closing Owner liability from the actual posted ledger, recorded card payments, reimbursable attribution, excluded personal spending, payments on behalf of customers and unmatched statement amounts. These columns have different meanings and must not be added together. Payable definitions are deduplicated across cards. Period-bound balances include actual posted corrections/reversals; source classification and statement-match cutoffs follow the requested period. Dates/timestamp cutoffs use UAE time. Historical inactive companies are readable when the existing financial scope permits; no activation is required to inspect them.

Payment rows retain their original source reference, card, purpose, invoice/client/vehicle references and source journal status. Personal rows remain source records, not company expenses or Owner receivables. Statement rows concern the card issuer's register company; payment splits concern their beneficiary company. The UI states that distinction instead of attributing a whole issuer statement to every beneficiary company.

Settlement rows show their original PV number, current physical-custody stage/delegate, and independently bounded accounting, deposit, card-repayment and final-cycle closure flags. The report explicitly distinguishes current workflow state from verification by period end. Original issuer-payment allocations are not summed as additional company expenses or receipts. Customer-linked payment rows open the existing receivables interface.

Only the actual current Owner's personal-card sources are included, after existing company report permission checks. Export/print permission is rechecked by the server; no permission or role is granted. A historical payable definition ambiguously shared with a different Owner blocks the report for mapping review, rather than leaking or guessing their balance. Fresh personal-card definitions are serialized and cannot be claimed by two different Owners. No historical definitions are changed automatically.

The Owner settlement register exposes a date-filtered report, Excel XML export and existing print/PDF preview. All spreadsheet cells are explicit strings with XML escaping; a source reference starting with `=` stays literal. The existing shared RTL/LTR styling and print shell are reused. Results are on-demand, company/date scoped and bounded; there is no new polling or subscription.

Acceptance: mixed 75 AED payment across two companies produces 65/10 recorded source totals, Owner liabilities of 60/10, personal exclusion of 5, customer disbursement of 20 and an independent unmatched statement of 25. A later empty period has 60 AED opening/closing liability and zero payment rows. A company-B report contains only its 10 AED split. The customer's 20 AED invoice stays open. General finance reporters are denied personal reports/exports. Ambiguous cross-Owner mappings fail closed. The full three-actor browser verified a settled cycle with zero remaining Owner liability, separate closure flags, real Excel download and PDF generation, in AR/EN at desktop/tablet/mobile widths. Four related native tests, TypeScript, 172 static checks and portable build passed; the final cross-Owner isolation refinement is separately recorded in BUILD-STATE.

No Supabase-original write, financial migration, Render change or deployment occurred. Other master-checklist work and full cumulative acceptance remain pending.
