# VAT ledger-control workpaper and corporate profit reconciliation — build only

The existing company tax draft, native chart/journals/approved bank operations, invoice credit procedure, private archive and report export/print shell are extended. No VAT or corporate-tax return is submitted, no invoice tax rate changes, and no original financial row is rewritten.

Corporate accounting-profit reconciliation previously selected only `finance_journals`, excluding real approved expenses/income carried by native bank operations. It now reads the same posted/approved entry sources as the actual company ledger. A synthetic 100 AED sale and approved 20 AED bank expense produce 80 AED accounting profit; an unapproved 30 AED operation and another company's 900 AED sale are excluded. The proposed tax bridge still exposes `taxableIncome:null`, `taxDue:null` and `filingReady:false` until substantive legal review; accounting profit is not taxable income by default.

An authorized operator can explicitly review independent output-liability and input-asset VAT controls inside the existing company tax draft. An optional archive proof and a reason are retained in the existing audit history. Customer/staff receivables, supplier/third-party/Owner/card liabilities, bank/fund/custody accounts, reciprocal accounts and registered prepaid wallets cannot be substituted as VAT controls. Workpapers revalidate current account separation, even if another configuration later drifts. Retired charts remain readable for history. Older tax-config clients preserve previously reviewed controls rather than erasing them by omission. This reporting review does not grant posting, recovery or filing authority.

The on-demand workpaper has exact opening, signed period movement and closing control balances, original posted source references and separate output/input rows. It includes actual approved bank operations and posted reversals/credits, excludes draft sources, and is company/date/AED scoped with bounded period details and aggregate opening queries. It does not calculate VAT from cash receipts, finance principal, custody, gross expenses or OCR suggestions. The control movement difference is explicitly labeled as a ledger difference, not net VAT due. Recoverable input tax and tax due stay unresolved; supply completeness is not certified and a zero input ledger does not certify zero recoverable VAT.

The company tax interface reuses the existing configuration/period form and adds reviewed control mapping plus workpaper preview, Excel and print/PDF. Report/export permissions are enforced on the server. Stale actor/company responses are not shown/downloaded. No extra polling, subscription, external delivery or provider connection is added.

Acceptance: four serial native tax/workpaper tests passed. A 105 AED original invoice with a 5 AED posted tax component and a 10.5 AED credit leaves 4.5 AED output movement; an approved 2 AED input-control entry is shown separately, while a draft 10 AED journal is excluded. The 2.5 AED movement difference is not called a tax assessment. A subsequent empty period retains 4.5/2 AED opening balances. Wrong receivable/payable control mappings, missing setup and unprivileged report/export access fail closed. Existing financial rows and grants stay unchanged. The actual AR/EN desktop/tablet/mobile browser passed setup, invoice/credit/input reconciliation, real Excel/PDF export and unchanged customer AR. An initial parallel local fixture launch collided while configuring shared test-cluster roles; the supported serial test mode passed without changing any production permission or database.

Remaining tax requirements: actual reviewed TRNs/legal registrations, supply classification and recovery eligibility, return-box completeness/reconciliation, corporate regime/adjustment approval, legal tax documents and the approved UAE electronic-invoicing/provider integration. These remain explicitly partial/unimplemented in the master checklist. A proposed label or a successful local workpaper is not official compliance. No source Supabase writes, migration, filing, historical conversion or deployment occurred.
