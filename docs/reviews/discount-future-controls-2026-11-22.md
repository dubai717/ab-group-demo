# E50 — future discount controls without changing the unlimited trial (BUILD ONLY)

Requirement 6.13 preserves the ability to add future limits/approval authority; it does not authorize imposing them during the current trial. The existing discount/collection route remains unlimited for the originally authorized employee unless a **separate specific Owner financial approval** selects one exact immutable proposal ID and digest under `discount_policy`. No UI/API in this package activates that financial feature, grants authority or writes that approval on a hosted project.

Owner-only company proposal versions use the original encrypted settings/audit/idempotency infrastructure. Each version records its future effective date, reason, cumulative invoice gross-credit employee amount/percentage limit and proposed cumulative Owner review threshold. At least one control is required. Versions cannot be updated/deleted through these routes; stale/concurrent versions are rejected. A new draft cannot replace an already reviewed version. Ordinary configuration/discount permissions do not grant policy management.

The existing workspace shows the inactive proposals and previews them against the actual original invoice amount, prior credits and outstanding balance. Exact integer arithmetic compares basis points and amounts. It does not edit the invoice, create a credit, collect money, allocate a receipt or post an entry. Source/preview company boundaries remain original; technical identifiers are not input by staff. AR/EN forms retain input errors and show no activation action.

Future `employee_limit_owner_review_v1` semantics are explicit: exceeding a reviewed staff limit/threshold requires the Owner to perform the original invoice correction under existing correction authority and record its reason. It is not a new approval task system. The checked exact proposal/digest, actor and cumulative result are audited within the same original transaction. It does not alter past credits, grant a new right, bypass original Work Amount/contract/write-off/currency/period/outstanding guards or change receipt/VAT treatment. Cancellation and other non-discount corrective document kinds do not acquire discount-policy semantics.

A feature approval with a missing, stale or invalid policy binding **fails closed**, rather than silently becoming unlimited. A correctly reviewed future-effective version imposes nothing before its effective date. Non-AED processing under this AED future policy requires review rather than currency conversion. Current trial operation (no such activation record) remains unchanged. Approval adoption and the final combined release remain separately protected Owner decisions, not performed here.
