# Prospective invoice credits use their original posted source — build only

An isolated regression reproduced two existing problems when legacy item settings differ from the original invoice: future credits recalculated original VAT from the **current** item rate, and a changed current AR mapping could redirect the credit to a different account.

For an original105.000 invoice with recorded5.000 VAT, changing only the synthetic legacy current rate from5 to10 caused credits of10.500,10.500 and84.000 to record0.955,0.955 and7.635 VAT. The corrected original-source path records0.500,0.500 and4.000, exhausting exactly the original recorded5.000 without changing that invoice or its journal. This reproduces a source-selection defect; it is not evidence that any particular hosted invoice is wrong and no historical correction was performed.

Before a future native credit, the existing correction endpoint now requires the unique unreversed Posted original invoice journal, matching invoice/item/company/currency metadata, its recorded tax fields and exact balanced original account entries. Current mapped AR, contra and tax charts must still correspond to that posting; a mismatch requires review instead of silently choosing either a new treatment or an old account. Recorded tax is used as accounting evidence, not legal certification or a newly selected VAT rate.

Prior posted credits must contain their original invoice/tax evidence. Remaining gross and tax must be consistent before the existing exact tax-portion allocator runs. The new credit journal records the original invoice journal and recorded rate as provenance. The original unlimited authorized trial discount policy, Owner notice, approval permissions, native document numbers and no-cash treatment remain unchanged. Classified investor-agency corrections keep their separate existing path. Unsupported legacy originals stop for review; their records are not adopted or rewritten.

Verification: the old source failed both reproduced cases. Three new native cases then passed: exact partial/final recorded-tax exhaustion and immutable original history; changed AR/income/VAT mappings refused with no new adjustment/journal; a synthetic legacy invoice without its source journal refused without reconstruction. Final34/34 targeted cases passed across correction, original discount policy, operational ledger, Owner-fronted customer payments, VAT workpaper and existing e-invoice source preparation. TypeScript and the original actual financial-package browser passed. All172 static cases and portable build/module-graph checks also passed.

All tests used an independently allocated loopback5443 PostgreSQL and fake providers. No original Supabase access, migration, historical financial rewrite, grant change, cash movement, delivery request or Render operation. This guard is prospective and does not claim that legal tax credit documents or all95 requirements are complete.
